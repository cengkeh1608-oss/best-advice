## WINNING FORMULA (from the three finals)
- ONE sentence, 90–185 chars (sweet spot ~100–180). One breath.
- ONE concrete domestic scene: a named person (Mum, grandmother, Ms. Chen, boss) + a specific object/ritual (banana dated "tomorrow", blanket folded back, voicemails, two cups, coffee half-finished, 11:40 draft) + a specific time/number (twenty years, 1987, 11:40/11:41, 40 seconds, March).
- Understated, deadpan, real voice recalling a real moment. NO slogan, NO fortune cookie, NO setup-punchline scaffolding, NO stated moral ("and that was the lesson" = filler).
- The twist IS the scene: the last clause lands quietly ("and every Sunday, I do" / "I'd keep a grocery list if your handwriting was on it").
- Same sponsor (u-pangg7) rewards the same taste in Wonder: concrete scene + specific detail + quiet landing.
- FOR THIS BOUNTY: the advice must be EMBODIED in a lived moment — a specific person, quoted or paraphrased in their own words, in a specific place, with a lived consequence. Generic wisdom ("never give up", "be yourself", "work hard") is a fortune cookie and loses to a lived scene. The advice itself must be specific enough to carry (criterion 2), not just the scene.
